# Unishine fact isolation

Treat Unishine as an independent brand, product portfolio, evidence system, and website. Similar appearance, naming, ownership, manufacturing, application, or technical direction does not prove facts are transferable.

| Claim type | Allowed evidence | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| Product specification | Latest Unishine catalog or exact official product page | Preserve model, option, unit, and condition. |
| Factory/manufacturing | Current Unishine factory or company page | Do not import Chipshow numbers or capabilities. |
| OEM/ODM | Explicit Unishine source | Do not promise customization, branding, MOQ, or engineering scope without evidence. |
| Warranty/service | Current Unishine service or warranty page | Preserve scope; dynamic commitments require confirmation. |
| Certification | Exact Unishine product/certificate evidence | Do not generalize from one product. |
| Price/stock/lead time/MOQ | Current task-specific confirmation | Block when not supplied. |
| Project/customer/case | Published Unishine case or approved user evidence | Never borrow a Chipshow case or customer. |
| Standards/regulations | Current primary external source | Do not claim Unishine compliance without product evidence. |

Before handoff, scan the draft and notes for `Chipshow`, `chipshow.com`, `chipshow.eu`, `chipshowledusa.com`, and Chipshow product names or claims. Remove any occurrence unless it is an explicit internal warning explaining why the evidence was rejected.
