1. Latest Unishine product catalog
2. Current official Unishine product page
3. Current official Unishine company, factory, quality, service, or FAQ page

## Conflicts and dynamic facts

- When the catalog and website conflict, mark the fact `blocked` and request confirmation.
- Recheck price, stock, MOQ, lead time, availability, delivery promises, service response, and warranty details for every task.
- Preserve the exact product, model, market, certificate, and scope of any certification claim.
- Do not generalize one product's optional feature or certification to the full range.

## External and forbidden sources

Use current primary sources only for standards, regulations, exhibitions, or market conditions. They cannot establish an Unishine product fact.

Never use Chipshow websites, catalogs, product cards, specifications, cases, certificates, patents, internal files, or prior Chipshow drafts.
